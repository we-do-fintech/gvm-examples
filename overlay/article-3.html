Article 3 — mangle-blur

Blur plus word shuffling for extra obfuscation.

The premium tier of the demo: 4.99 PLN with the mangle-blur strategy.

The mangle-blur strategy blurs the hidden content and additionally shuffles the word order.

This makes casual copying from the page harder, at the cost of a slightly more confusing preview.

It is a good middle ground for publishers who want obfuscation without a full proxy/backend setup.

Like the other pages, the configuration lives only in the tenant config (wdft_showcase.json).

On payment, the original content is restored and the shuffled text is replaced with the real paragraphs.

Every article in this demo shares the same page skeleton — only the data-gvm-reference differs.
