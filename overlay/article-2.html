Article 2 — hard hide

The hide strategy removes content instead of blurring it.

Cheaper price (0.75 PLN), different strategy — the only difference is a config entry.

Here the intro stays visible, but everything after the threshold is removed and replaced by the paywall template.

This article uses the hide strategy: hidden sections are removed from the DOM until payment succeeds.

It is the simplest strategy and the easiest to reason about, though also the easiest to bypass with dev tools.

The price is set to 0.75 PLN purely to show that each article can carry its own value.

After a successful payment the removed sections are restored exactly where they were.

Try ?gvm_admin=1 to switch this article to blur and save — reload and see it change.
