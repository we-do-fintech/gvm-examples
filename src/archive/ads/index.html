The one-złoty reader: how regional newsrooms are pricing the scroll

Publishers spent a decade chasing subscriptions. A smaller, faster experiment is winning the readers who will never subscribe — and it starts with a single honest price.

By Marta Zielińska · 3 Mar 2026 · 6 min read

For most of the last decade the answer to “how do we pay for this?” was a subscription. The subscription worked for the readers who already loved the paper, and did almost nothing for everybody else. The readers who arrived once a week from a search result or a shared link were never going to commit to a month, a year, or a card number.

The experiment that changed the maths was almost embarrassingly small. Instead of asking those readers for a commitment, the paper asked them for pocket change — and let them pay on the phone bill they already had.

What surprised the editors was not that readers paid. It was how little friction mattered. A single SMS, no redirect to an app store, no password, no card. The reader reached the end of the free preview, tapped once, and the rest of the story unblurred in place.

The same plumbing that unlocks an article can also sell a quieter promise: a page without banners. Publishers have started offering it as a day pass — a small, honest payment that buys twenty-four hours of clean reading on the device in front of you. No plan, no renewal, no awkward cancellation.

The commercial lesson is the same in both cases. Readers do not hate paying for news. They hate being asked for the wrong thing — a commitment when all they wanted was one story, or a subscription when all they wanted was a quiet page.

Developer Kit

Ads disappear after one redirect

The pass button uses the redirect strategy: after a resolved payment gvm.js sends the reader back with signed query params, and the page hides every .ad-slot.

Minimal ad-free snippet

Source on GitHub | gvm.js documentation

Redirect to a page without ads 
<!-- 1) Inject gvm.js SDK -->
<script src="https://esm.sh/@wdft/gvm-sdk@latest/gvm.js" type="module"></script>

<!-- 2) Initialize gvm on body -->
<body data-gvm data-gvm-tenant="your_tenant" data-gvm-env="qa">
  <!-- 3) Declare payment template (modal) — mandatory consent checkbox -->
  <template data-gvm-template="payment" data-gvm-template-inject-to="body">
    <dialog open>
      <p>Dostęp do tej Treści jest odpłatny: <strong><span data-gvm-bind-price></span>&nbsp;<span data-gvm-bind-currency></span></strong>
        zostanie doliczone do zbiorczego rozliczenia Twojej Sesji. Sublicencja do Treści wygasa z chwilą Płatności.
        Szczegóły w <a href="https://getviamsg.com/terms" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Regulaminie</a>.</p>
      <label><input type="checkbox" data-gvm-accept-terms="[data-gvm-bind-send-sms]"
             data-gvm-accept-terms-version="pl-2026-10-23" /><span>Żądam rozpoczęcia świadczenia…</span></label>
      <button data-gvm-bind-send-sms>Send SMS</button>
      <button data-gvm-bind-cancel>Cancel</button>
    </dialog>
  </template>

  <!-- 4) Paid trigger: redirect back with ?noads=1 on success -->
  <button data-gvm-price="1.49"
          data-gvm-reference="ads_free_day"
          data-gvm-template-name="payment"
          data-gvm-http-redirect-to="/archive/ads/?noads=1">Remove ads for a day</button>

  <!-- 5) On landing, read gvm-status + noads and hide .ad-slot for 24h -->
</body>
Environment: the page uses data-gvm-env="qa" with tenant wdft_showcase_qa, so the SMS/QR flow runs against the QA API without a real charge. On a resolved payment gvm.js redirects to /ads/?noads=1 with gvm-status=resolved; the page stores the day in localStorage and removes every ad slot before the first paint. Open the console to watch the gvm_* events.
